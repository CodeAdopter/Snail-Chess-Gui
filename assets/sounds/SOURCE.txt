The board's sounds and the clock's for Snail Chess GUI: move.wav, capture.wav, castle.wav,
clock_press.wav and clock_ticks.wav, a loop. The program embeds them, and tools/sounds.py made
them from the recordings below.

Free for anyone to use. These sounds and the recordings they were made from are in the public
domain under CC0 1.0 Universal, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Use them in your own projects, free or sold, change them and share them. No credit is needed and
there is no one to ask.

The recordings are by JJTaynos, https://freesound.org/people/JJTaynos/, who released them under
CC0 on freesound.org. tools/sounds.py reads a few seconds of each from source/, a folder not kept
here, each named for the second of the recording it starts at:

    chess-pieces-move-far-24s.wav   "Chess Pieces Move (Far)"   https://freesound.org/s/733932/
    clock-press-close-7s.wav        "CLOCK PRESS Close-T001"    https://freesound.org/s/733930/
    clock-press-close-41s.wav       "CLOCK PRESS Close-T001"    https://freesound.org/s/733930/
    clock-tick-far-39s.wav          "CLOCK TICK Far-T002"       https://freesound.org/s/733929/

What this project made from them, the cuts, pitches, tone, room, the capture's two knocks and the
clock's loop, its author dedicates to the public domain under CC0 1.0 as well.
