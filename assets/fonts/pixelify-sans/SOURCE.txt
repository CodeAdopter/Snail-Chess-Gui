Pixelify Sans, from Google Fonts (fonts.google.com/specimen/Pixelify+Sans).
regular.ttf is weight 400 and strong.ttf weight 600, static instances served by the Google Fonts CSS API,
cut by tools/fonts.py to the characters Snail Chess GUI's own text uses, without hinting or glyph names.
