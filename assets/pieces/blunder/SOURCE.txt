Blunder pieces, Snail Chess GUI's own, drawn by tools/piece_sets/blunder.py, the animated SVGs. tools/pieces.py
reads their CSS animations as motions and stands them in the square as it stands every set. A file named for a
mood, as wP-promoting, wK-checked or wK-mated, is that piece in the mood, standing where the calm piece stands.
